# Security

## What this extension can see and do

It has no server and no analytics, and it sends nothing to its authors. It stores nothing.

- **Access to `meet.google.com`.** To read the text of Meet's buttons on the page and press the admit button. It doesn't read or keep the contents of your call, your chat or the names of the people in it.
- **`activeTab`.** To read the address of the Meet tab when you click the icon, so it can open that call in Companion mode. It gets this only for the tab you clicked, and only at that moment.

## Keep in mind

It admits everyone who asks to join, and that includes people you don't know if they have the link. Turn it off for a call where you want to choose who joins.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please don't open a public issue for a security problem. Use GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button on this repository, with steps to reproduce and the version you use.

We will acknowledge the report, work on a fix, and credit you in the release notes unless you prefer otherwise.
