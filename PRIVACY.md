# Auto Admit for Google Meet Privacy Policy

English · [繁體中文](#繁體中文)

Effective date: 2026-11-03

This policy covers the Auto Admit for Google Meet browser extension (線上會議自動接受工具), published by V-AX.

## What the extension handles

- **Website content:** on `meet.google.com` only, the text of the buttons on the page, so it can find and press the admit button, the button that opens the list of people waiting, and the Companion mode button. A button's text can include the name of someone asking to join. The extension only checks whether the text starts with a word such as "Admit"; it doesn't keep it. It doesn't read your call's audio or video, your chat, or the content of any other site.
- **The address of the Meet tab:** only when you click the extension's icon on that tab, to open the same call in a new tab in Companion mode.

## Where it is kept

Nothing is stored. The extension has no storage and keeps nothing after the page closes. The Companion mode tab is a normal tab, so its address can stay in your browser history like any other page.

## What is sent, and to whom

The extension has no server and no analytics. It sends nothing to V-AX or to anyone else, and it makes no network requests of its own. Pressing Meet's buttons is the same as you pressing them yourself.

## What we don't do

We don't sell your data, transfer it to third parties, use it for anything other than the extension's single purpose, or use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Questions: support@v-ax.com, or an issue at https://github.com/xxxxDev/auto-admit-meet/issues.

## 繁體中文

生效日期：2026-11-03

本政策適用於 V-AX 發布的線上會議自動接受工具（Auto Admit for Google Meet）瀏覽器擴充工具。

### 擴充工具會處理的資料

- **網站內容：** 只在 `meet.google.com`，讀取網頁上按鈕的文字，用來找到並按下「接受」、打開等待名單和「使用夥伴模式」的按鈕。按鈕文字可能包含要求加入者的名字；擴充工具只檢查文字開頭是不是「接受」這類字，不會保留。不讀取通話的聲音或畫面、聊天內容，也不讀取其他網站的內容。
- **Meet 分頁的網址：** 只在你點擴充工具圖示時讀取該分頁的網址，用來在新分頁以夥伴模式開啟同一場通話。

### 存放位置

不儲存任何資料。擴充工具沒有使用儲存空間，網頁關閉後什麼都不留。夥伴模式開在一般分頁，所以網址可能和其他網頁一樣留在瀏覽紀錄裡。

### 傳送哪些資料、傳給誰

擴充工具沒有伺服器，也沒有使用分析，不會傳任何資料給 V-AX 或其他人，自己也不發出任何網路請求。它按下 Meet 的按鈕，和你自己按一樣。

### 我們不做的事

我們不販售你的資料、不轉移給第三方、不用於擴充工具單一用途以外的目的，也不用於判斷信用或借貸。

### 變更與聯絡

本政策如有變更，新版會公布在同一個網址並更新生效日期。如有問題請寫信到 support@v-ax.com，或在 https://github.com/xxxxDev/auto-admit-meet/issues 提出。
